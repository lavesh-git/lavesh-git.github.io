Huffman Coding

  - Data Compression like file compression ZIP, and media compression JPEG

  - Huffman Coding is an algorithm that assigns variable length codes to input characters based on their frequencies, with the most frequent characters getting the shortest codes. The goal is to minimize the overall size of encoded data.

  - The greedy approach work by always combining the two least frequent characters first (local optimum), ensuring that each step reduces the size of the overall encoding (global optimum)
