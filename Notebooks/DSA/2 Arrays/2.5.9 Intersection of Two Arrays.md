# 2.5.9 Intersection of Two Arrays

EXTENSION: [Find Union and Intersection of two unsorted arrays - GeeksforGeeks](<https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/find-union-and-intersection-of-two-unsorted-arrays/?ref=ml_lbp>)

BRUTE:

TC: O(n1\*n2)

SC: O(n2)

OPTIMAL:

if both match, then add in list and move both, else keep moving one

TC: O(n1 + n2)

SC: O(n1 + n2) for ans array
