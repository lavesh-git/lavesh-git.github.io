# 2.3 Relational Databases

- Databases are most critical and brittle component of a system. They make or break a system. If we solve our database problem really well, then our system will be very stable.

![Diagram](assets/image-2f469052a0499ef6ebc2.png)

- Data is stored and represented in rows and columns. They take inspiration from **ledgers (spreadsheets and balance sheets) **which were in rows and column wise manner.
