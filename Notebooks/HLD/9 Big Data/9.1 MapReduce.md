# 9.1 MapReduce

A typical big data pipeline looks like this:

  1. **Distributed Consensus** (e.g., via ZooKeeper) ensures the "Job Tracker" is alive and healthy.

  1. The user submits a **Batch Processing** job.

  1. The system uses **MapReduce** to split the job into thousands of tasks.

  1. If the job requires combining two datasets, it performs a **Data Join** during the shuffle-and-sort phase of the MapReduce cycle.
